Majeh's Email Browserr

A secure, local way to browse your inbox.

What this app does

Majeh's Email Browserr is an intuitive dashboard designed to let users easily read and browse their Gmail inbox. The application requires read-only access to display your message subjects, senders, and email bodies directly within the interface.

  • A fast three-pane mail client: inbox, message list and reading pane.
  • Read-only Google sign-in — the app can never send, delete or modify your mail.
  • Your tokens are encrypted locally in a vault opened by your own password.
  • Fetched mail is cached on your device and can be exported or re-imported as a JSON backup.

Data we access, and why

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly — read the message subjects, senders and bodies you ask the app to display.
  • email — your Google email address, used only to label your account in the sidebar.
  • profile — your display name and picture, shown next to your account.
  • openid — your Google account identity, used only to link the sign-in to your encrypted vault.

100% Private & Serverless Architecture

Your security is our priority. Majeh's Email Browserr operates entirely on the client side within your local web browser, hosted as a static web app via Firebase Hosting. All email fetches using the gmail.readonly API are processed locally on your device. We do not own, maintain, or utilize an external database server, and your private email data is never tracked or transmitted to any third party. Your authentication tokens and fetched email data are held only in your own browser — the tokens encrypted in a local vault, the mail in your browser's local storage — and are deleted by you at any time.

How to get started

  1. Create your vault — open Settings and set a vault password under “Connected Accounts”.
  2. Connect Gmail — in Settings, tap “Connect Gmail” and sign in with Google.
  3. Read your mail — pick your account in the sidebar, then use “Fetch by date” for older mail.
