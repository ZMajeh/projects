Privacy Policy

Majeh's Email Browserr · Last updated: 21 October 2026

Short version: this app has no backend and no analytics. Your emails, tokens and settings stay in your browser. The only data that leaves your device is sent directly to Google, because that is where your mailbox lives.

1. Who we are

Majeh's Email Browserr ("the App") is a personal, client-side web application for reading email. It is operated by the individual developer who distributes it. For any privacy question, contact zmajeh@gmail.com.

2. The App is client-side only

The App is a static website. It contains no application server, no database, and no account system of its own. It is hosted on Firebase Hosting, which serves static files only - Firebase does not receive a copy of your mail, and the App has no server-side component that could collect it.

There is no analytics, telemetry, advertising, or user tracking in the App. No cookies are set by the App, and no third-party trackers are embedded.

3. What the App stores, and where

Everything the App keeps is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device:

DataWhere it livesProtection
OAuth access & refresh tokens localStorage, in an encrypted "vault" Encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is derived from your vault password using PBKDF2 (100,000 iterations). It never leaves your device.
Fetched emails (headers, bodies, dates, read/starred state) localStorage, per account Stored in plain text on your device. Anyone with access to your device or browser profile can read it. Use the "Delete Downloaded Emails" control to clear it at any time.
Google OAuth client ID / client secret localStorage (client secret) and the page markup (client ID) Stored locally so you can supply your own credentials. The client secret is never committed to source control.
Vault password Memory only Kept in memory for the current tab session and never written to disk.

4. What leaves your device

The App talks directly to Google, and only to Google:

  • Google OAuth sign-in - so you can grant permission to read your mailbox.
  • Gmail API - to list and download the messages you ask for.
  • Firebase Hosting - to deliver the App's own static files.

Google processes that data under its own privacy policy and terms. The App does not proxy, copy, log or analyse your mail on any server it controls.

5. How your data is used (limited use)

Data accessed by the App is used solely to provide the App's own functionality - namely, displaying the messages you ask the App to retrieve from your own mailbox, on your own device, back to you. Specifically:

  • Your email data is never sold, rented, traded or licensed.
  • Your email data is never shared with advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers or any other third party.
  • Your email data is never used to build profiles, to target advertising, or to train any model.
  • The App contains no advertising of any kind.
  • No data is transmitted to, or processed by, any server operated by the developer. There is no backend database to collect it in the first place.

6. Permissions the App requests

The App requests read-only access to your Gmail mailbox (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly) plus your basic profile (name, email address, profile picture). It cannot send, delete, or modify your email, and it cannot read anything outside the mailbox you authorise.

7. Your choices and controls

  • Revoke access - remove the App from your Google account connections. This immediately stops all access.
  • Export your data - the Tokens page can export your encrypted vault, and the email cache can be downloaded as a JSON backup.
  • Delete your data - use "Delete Downloaded Emails" to clear cached mail, "Clear" next to the client secret to remove it, and clear your browser's site data to remove everything the App has stored, including the vault.

8. Security

The App is served over HTTPS. Your vault is encrypted locally with a key derived from your password via PBKDF2, and the password is never stored. As with any software that keeps secrets on a device, a compromised device or browser profile can expose locally stored data - keep your operating system and browser up to date, and use a strong, unique vault password.

9. Children

The App is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact zmajeh@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

If the App's data practices change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change. Continuing to use the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
